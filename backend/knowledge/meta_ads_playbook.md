# Nigeria & Global Meta Ads Playbook (Expert Rules)

## 1. Market Context: Running Meta Ads in Nigeria 🇳🇬
Always tailor your strategies, budgets, and ad copy for the **Nigerian market** (unless I specifically say the client is abroad):
- Use **Naira (₦)** for all budgets and pricing examples (e.g., ₦5,000 to ₦15,000/day starting test budget).
- Understand **Nigerian Buyer Psychology**: Trust is the #1 barrier in Nigeria ("What I ordered vs. What I got"). Ads MUST emphasize proof, real videos/photos, physical location/address (if local), customer reviews, or clear delivery terms (e.g., Payment on Delivery in Lagos/Abuja, or verified business credibility).

## 2. Best Campaign Objectives for Nigeria (ODAX)
Meta Ads Manager ONLY has 6 objectives: Sales, Leads, Engagement, Traffic, Awareness, App Promotion.
- **For Service Businesses & High-Ticket Offers in Nigeria (Real Estate, Clinics, Agencies, B2B):**
  - Use the **Leads** objective (choosing either **WhatsApp**, **Instant Forms**, or **Instagram Direct**) OR **Engagement** objective (set to **Messaging Apps: WhatsApp & Instagram DM**).
  - Why? Nigerians strongly prefer chatting on **WhatsApp** or **Instagram DM** to ask questions and verify you are real before paying.
- **For E-Commerce / Physical Products in Nigeria:**
  - If using a website with a Lead/Order form (Payment on Delivery): Use the **Sales** or **Leads** objective optimizing for Website Conversions.
  - If selling directly in DMs: Use **Engagement (Messaging Apps)** or **Leads (WhatsApp/Instagram)**, and always include the **Price in Naira (₦)** inside the ad copy so broke/unqualified clickers don't waste your WhatsApp time!
- **NEVER use "Traffic" or "Awareness"** expecting sales or leads. They only bring cheap clicks from people who never buy.

## 3. Nigerian Targeting Strategy
- **Location Targeting:**
  - Never target "All of Nigeria" unless selling a cheap digital product nationwide.
  - Focus on high-purchasing-power states/cities where you can actually deliver: **Lagos** (often separating Lagos Island: Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Ajah from Mainland: Ikeja, Surulere, Yaba, Magodo), **Abuja (FCT)**, **Port Harcourt (Rivers)**, **Ibadan (Oyo)**, **Enugu**, or **Kano** depending on the business.
  - For a local walk-in business (like a dentist, spa, salon, or restaurant in Lekki or Ikeja): Target specific neighborhoods or a 5km–15km radius around the shop.
- **Audience & Qualifying:**
  - Because many people in Nigeria click ads just to ask "How much?" without money to buy, use **Qualifying Signals**:
  - Put the starting price (e.g., "From ₦25,000") clearly in the ad copy or headline to filter out time-wasters.
  - Test **Broad Targeting** (Location + Age 24–55) vs. **High-Purchasing-Power Interest Stacks** (e.g., Engaged Shoppers, Frequent International Travelers, Business Owners, Real Estate, or specific device/income signals).

## 4. High-Converting Nigerian Ad Copy Framework
Never write boring corporate grammar. Write clear, relatable, trust-building copy in 4 parts:
1. **The Callout Hook (Line 1):** Call out the exact city or problem immediately (e.g., "Lagos Business Owners!" or "Staying in Abuja and looking for...").
2. **The Value & Clear Offer (in ₦):** State the exact result and the offer in Naira (e.g., "Get Professional Teeth Whitening in Ikeja for ₦35,000 instead of ₦60,000 this week!").
3. **Trust & Proof (Crucial in Nigeria):** Mention your physical office area, years of experience, customer reviews, or delivery guarantee so they know it is not a scam.
4. **Clear Call To Action (CTA):** Tell them the exact next step (e.g., "Click 'Send WhatsApp Message' below to book your slot now!").

## 5. Meta Ad Policy Compliance
- **Personal Attributes Policy:** NEVER use "You/Your" to imply a personal or medical flaw. Focus on the positive result.
