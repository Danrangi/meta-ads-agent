# Meta Ads Performance Analysis & Troubleshooting Guide (Nigeria & Global)

STRICT RULE: When I give you my campaign numbers, ONLY analyze the exact 1 campaign I gave you. Never invent "Campaign #2" or "Campaign #3".

Diagnose my campaign step-by-step using these 3 rules:

## 1. Check the Outbound CTR (Click-Through Rate)
- **Rule:** Outbound CTR should be at least **1.0%** (ideally **1.5% to 3.0%+** in Nigeria).
- **Diagnosis if CTR is BELOW 1.0%:** Your **Ad Creative (Video/Image) or Line 1 Hook** is weak. People are scrolling past your ad without clicking.
- **Fix:** Do NOT touch targeting yet. Test a new video/image and a stronger opening line calling out your target audience.

## 2. Check the Conversion after the Click (Leads / WhatsApp Chats / Sales)
- **Diagnosis if CTR is ABOVE 1.5% (Good!), but Leads/Sales are 0 or too expensive:**
  - Your ad creative and hook are working great! Do NOT change the image or headline.
  - The bottleneck is happening **after the click**:
    1. **If using a Website/Landing Page:** The page may be loading too slowly on Nigerian mobile data (MTN/Airtel), or the offer/price seems untrustworthy.
    2. **If using WhatsApp / Instagram DM:** Either people are clicking the button but not sending the pre-filled WhatsApp message, OR your WhatsApp closing script is too slow (you must reply within 5 minutes!).
    3. **If getting lots of WhatsApp chats who say "I'll get back to you" and never buy:** You forgot to put the **Price (₦)** in the ad copy, so your ad is attracting broke window-shoppers. Add the price to the ad to filter for serious buyers!

## 3. Check for Creative Fatigue
- If an ad worked great for 2–3 weeks and suddenly costs doubled, check **Frequency**. If Frequency is over **3.0**, the audience is tired of seeing the same video. Keep the targeting and launch a fresh video creative.
